 📊 Sales & Revenue Analysis Dashboard

 📌 Project Overview
The Sales & Revenue Analysis Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project developed to analyze business performance through sales, profit, orders, and customer insights.  
The dashboard provides a clear understanding of revenue trends, top-performing products, category performance, and regional sales distribution using dynamic visualizations and KPI tracking.

 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and revenue performance
- Track important KPIs and business metrics
- Identify top-performing products and categories
- Compare sales performance across regions
- Enable interactive business analysis using slicers and filters

 🚀 Key Features
- 📈 KPI Cards for Total Sales, Profit, Orders, and Quantity
- 📊 Sales Trend Analysis over time
- 🥧 Category-wise Sales Distribution
- 📍 Region-wise Sales Performance
- 🏆 Top Products by Sales Analysis
- 🎛 Interactive Filters and Slicers
- 🎨 Clean and professional dashboard design

 🛠 Tools & Technologies Used
- **Power BI– Dashboard creation and data visualization
- **Microsoft Excel– Dataset management
- **Data Analysis Techniques– KPI tracking and business insights

 📌 Key Business Insights
- Technology category contributes the highest sales among all categories.
- Canon imageCLASS is the top-performing product based on revenue.
- The West region generates strong overall sales performance.
- Consumer segment contributes the majority of total revenue.
- Sales show fluctuations across different time periods and years.
